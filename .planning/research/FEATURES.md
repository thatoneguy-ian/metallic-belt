# Features Research

## Table Stakes (Must Have)
- Click a roll button on D&D Beyond → Dice roll appears in Foundry VTT chat.
- Change HP on D&D Beyond → HP updates in Foundry VTT token/actor.
- Change HP in Foundry VTT → HP updates on D&D Beyond sheet.
- Initial character import (stats, spells, inventory).

## Differentiators
- Syncing conditions and exhaustion levels.
- Syncing spell slot usage bi-directionally.
- Pushing custom Foundry macros back to D&D Beyond.

## Anti-Features (Do Not Build)
- Full map/VTT rendering inside D&D Beyond (too heavy, redundant).
- Replacing Foundry's combat tracker with D&D Beyond's (keep VTT mechanics in VTT).
